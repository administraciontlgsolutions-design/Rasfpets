# Surface Brief: app/page.tsx (Conversion Landing Page)

## Scope & Mode
- **Scope**: Whole surface (`app/page.tsx`)
- **Mode**: Persuade
- **Audience**: Pet owners (dogs and cats) seeking natural, high-grade nutrition, tailored subscription or direct consultation.
- **Job**: Evaluate natural food benefits, configure pet profile in interactive selector, and start purchase or personalized consultation via WhatsApp.
- **Constraints**: Mobile-first (375px+), single primary accent `#ec101d` for CTAs, single H1, GSAP with clean context cleanup and prefers-reduced-motion support, no broken visual containers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Natural pet nutrition presented with scientific honesty, warm editorial craft, and zero friction, refusing the generic multi-color SaaS clutter and crowded supermarket retail grids.

OWN-WORLD: Organic warm ground (#fffaf8), deep charcoal typographic ink (#181313), pure milk card surfaces (#ffffff) with subtle warm borders (#eadfdb), and high-energy single-role crimson (#ec101d) strictly reserved for conversion triggers.

STORY: Visitor discovers natural nutrition advantages, validates trust metrics (+10k pets, 24h dispatch, 30-day warranty), customizes food via interactive 3-step quiz, inspects formula details, reads peer proofs, and converts to direct consultation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand header with compact consultation action. Hero grid featuring strong left-aligned typography ("Nutrición natural hecha a su medida"), value proof points, prominent pulsing red CTA, and a photography card anchoring real product and companion imagery.

FORM: Persuasion narrative sequence (Hero → Trust counters → Quiz selector → Benefits → Product detail → Proof/Testimonials → Comparison matrix → FAQ accordion → Final guarantee CTA → Footer).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
